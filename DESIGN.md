# Design: Femia storefront

World: **rose & champagne.** A sunlit boutique: ivory walls warming into blush, rosewood and rose-gold accents and the fine gold thread of zari embroidery. Couture-magazine type, soft daylight, quiet luxury. Chosen by the owner on 16 Nov 2026, replacing the earlier maroon "silk & zari" look.

**Premium principles (16 Nov 2026).** Restraint reads as expensive. One signature motif (the zari thread: menu stitch, margins, gold hairlines, and the thread the category swing tags hang on); flat ivory page and flat rosewood buttons (the hero wall is the one gradient moment; the rosewood footer the one dark band); 4px corners on photos and cards, pills only for buttons and tags; one section rhythm (64px phones → 112px wide screens each side, `.section`; tighter under the hero, 40px from a heading to its content); one section-heading size; quiet commerce (one sentence-case tag per piece; no % off or sale countdowns on the homepage); slow, small hover changes; photos fade in over their placeholder colour (`scripts/img-fade.ts`).

## Type
- Display: **Cormorant Garamond** (variable), weights 500–600, italic 500 for the accent phrase. A soft, refined Garamond: elegant without Bodoni's hard hairlines. Tracking −0.015em; hero max 6rem, page headings max 4rem. Serif is for headings only (≥ ~1.25rem); never for product names, cart lines or other small text.
- Text & UI: **DM Sans** (variable). Body 1rem/1.6, 400. Product names: DM Sans 500, 1rem. Uppercase tracked labels only in the desktop menu; buttons, tags and everything else in sentence case. Text links and form labels: sentence case.
- Hover and selection never change the typeface, style or size: only colour, underline or a soft highlight.
- Numerals: DM Sans tabular for prices.

## Colour
**Rose & champagne** (chosen 16 Nov 2026, replacing ivory & caramel). Defined in `apps/storefront/src/styles/global.css` as overrides of the shared tokens plus `--grad-*` gradients (Admin keeps the tokens' maroon).
- Ground: the page drifts from ivory `#FBF7F4` into blush `#F7E8E4` down its length (`--grad-page`); white surfaces `#FFFFFF`; soft band `#F8EEEA → #F3E3DF` (`--grad-band`: trust strip, boutique); sand `#F1E2DD`; line `#EDDFDA`.
- Ink `#33231F`, text `#4E3B37`, muted `#76605B`.
- Accent rosewood `#8E4A43` (links, italic accents, active states; 6.6:1 on ivory); rose gold `#B76E5D`; buttons a rose-gold gradient `#B76E5D → #8E4A43` with ivory text (`--grad-button`).
- Zari gold `#C8A574` (light `#E6CFA4`, deep `#94703F`) stays for thread only: hairlines, rings, grommets, the zari thread; never large fills.
- Announcement bar: champagne → rose → champagne. Hero: the boutique-wall photo, warming from champagne to rose on the rail's side.
- Footer: rosewood gradient `#5A2E2C → #3E1F1F` with the light wordmark, ivory text and a gold hairline on top (the shared tokens are re-mapped inside `.ft`).
- Shadows are tinted rose-brown.

## Material
- **Zari thread:** 1px gold hairlines; buttons carry an inner gold thread 3px inside the edge.
- **Warm depth:** layered shadows tinted warm brown with real offset and blur. Card radius 14px; white cards on the ivory ground.
- **The rail (hero):** the boutique's rail of new pieces. The rail runs from the middle of the hero off the right edge of the screen (the brass rod, rings and hooks were retired in the premium pass; the photos sit flush); the owner's photo comes first, open, and the four newest in-stock pieces hang beside it as narrow slices with their names running up the side like garment tags. Pointing at or tabbing to a piece slides it open (name and price appear) while the others close up. Headline, intro, buttons, tagline and live open/closed line sit on the left, vertically centred. Phones: headline, then the rail as a swipeable strip (photo card first), then the buttons, intro and status. Behind it, the boutique wall: a soft sunlit-plaster photo with window light and leaf shadows on the rail's side (`public/brand/hero-wall-v2.webp`, phones and tablets under 900px `hero-wall-phone-v2.webp`), an ivory wash over the copy side so text always reads, and a fade into the page at the foot.
- **Section heads:** heading on the left (one size, `--fs-section`), an optional quiet text link ("View all →", gold underline) on the right, on one baseline. No ornament.
- **Header:** white bar, logo left, uppercase menu centred (Home, Shop, Collections, About, Visit Us), search / account / wishlist / bag right. As you scroll it lifts off the page into a floating frosted capsule with a gold hairline and soft rose shadow (scroll-linked, reverses). Under the menu a short rosewood running stitch ending in a gold lozenge rests under the current page; pointing at another item pulls it across (stretches over both, then settles), and it travels to the new item on navigation.
  - **Mega panels** (Shop, Collections): open on hover (with intent delay) or keyboard focus, Esc closes; a frosted card with a gold hairline dropping from the capsule edges. Shop: category photos (3:4) with Cormorant names and "Shop everything", plus "Just in" (three newest pieces with price). Collections: three featured collection cards with descriptions, plus the full list.
  - **Search in the bar:** the search icon (or "/") turns the menu into a pill search field with instant results (photo, name, price, "See all results"); Esc or clicking away closes it. Phones keep the full-screen search sheet.
  - **Previews:** account (signed in: hello, orders, addresses, wishlist, track, sign out; signed out: sign-in button, track, wishlist) and bag (first three lines, subtotal, View bag / Checkout; empty state with "Shop new arrivals").
  - **Phones and tablets (< 1024px):** menu and search left, logo centred, account right; the menu opens a left drawer (native modal dialog) with category photos, collections, boutique pages, the live open/closed line and socials; wishlist and bag live in the tab bar.
- **App tab bar (phones and tablets):** fixed to the bottom, 64px plus the safe area, frosted white with a hairline and a soft upward shadow: Home, Shop (hanger), Collections, Wishlist (count), Bag (count; opens the cart, and added pieces fly to it). The current tab has a soft caramel pill behind its icon and a caramel label. Everything fixed to the bottom (toasts, the product buy bar, the footer's last row) sits above it via `--tabbar-h`; checkout hides it.
- **Categories (swing tags, the owner's favourite; keep):** each category is a white garment swing tag (cut top corners, brass grommet round a real punched eyelet), photo on the tag (4px corners) and the name below in Cormorant 1.5rem. The tags hang on a fine zari thread that sags across the section, so they hang lower towards the middle and the thread shows through each eyelet. They drop onto the thread and swing to rest as the section scrolls in (springy overshoot) and sway gently about the eyelet when pointed at. Phones and tablets: a swipeable row on a straight thread.
- **Product sections (the gallery):** a fashion-house gallery. Heading left, "View all" text link right; four large 3:4 photos (4px corners, no card box, no shadow) with the name (DM Sans 500) and price beneath (sale price rosewood, old price struck; no % off or countdown on the homepage) and small colour dots. One quiet sentence-case tag per piece (live offer, else New, or Sold out); the shop's product cards follow the same rule. Hover (pointer devices): crossfade to the second photo if there is one and a slow 3% zoom, a slim ivory "Add to bag" bar rises from the photo's foot, the heart fades in. Touch: heart and a round bag button always visible. Under 1024px: a swipe row through every piece. Page scroll is never held.
- **Styled by You (the table spread):** customers' tagged photos as straight, gallery-hung prints (8px white mat, soft shadow) spread around the heading on wide screens: the heading and "Wearing Femia? Tag us at @handle to be featured here." sit in the clear middle (35–65% of a 2.4:1 stage); the prints (up to eight) split into a left and a right group that hug it, each cascading across its side, alternating high and low and overlapping a little, . The layout is computed for the number of prints, and fewer prints are drawn bigger (one per side 22% wide, two 19%, three 16.5%, four 14.5%), so the table never looks empty. Pointing at a print straightens it, lifts it to the front and shows its caption (Cormorant italic on the photo's foot); prints link to their Instagram post when one is set. Phones: heading and invitation on top, then a two-column spread of prints at gentle angles.
- **Boutique (visit us):** an editorial two-column section inside the page width. Left: the boutique photo in an arched doorway frame (4px bottom corners, a fine gold line just inside the arch) that opens gently from narrow to full width as the section scrolls in. Right, left-aligned: the heading, a line of copy, Address and Hours side by side under a hairline (small muted labels), the live open/closed line, then one button (Get directions) with Chat on WhatsApp as a quiet text link. Phones: the photo (square), then the text. Used on the home page (falls back to the Visit page's boutique photo when the section has none) and as the Visit page's opening.
- **Promo banner ("the hem"):** the photo runs to one edge of the screen and ends in a straight seam with a dashed zari stitch just inside it. The heading is laid across the seam: ivory (italic in zari gold) over the photo, ink (italic in caramel) on the wall. On phones the photo is full width, the hem runs along its foot and the first heading line sits half on it. "Text position" in Admin picks the side the message sits on.
- **Trust strip:** one quiet line above the footer: each title (ink) and detail (muted), separated by fine gold hairlines; no icons. Phones: a 2×2 grid of short lines.
- **Footer:** light (`#F6F0E7`): logo, italic tagline, live open status and round social icons; Shop / Help / About; newsletter with a pill field and round caramel arrow.

## Motion
- Focal moment: the hero. The garments drop onto the rail one after another (clip from the top, photos settling from a slight zoom) while the headline lines rise and the rest fades in. Afterwards only the visitor moves it: hovering a piece slides it open along the rail.
- Supporting: the tailor's thread: a gold zari running stitch, led by a needle at the reading line, comes out from under the hero and runs down the side margins (never across text); it crosses the page only in the empty gaps between sections, draping across in one soft curve; above the footer it ties a small knot (paisleys and the needle were retired in the premium pass); the category tags drop onto their thread and swing to rest as the section scrolls in (springy overshoot), and swing gently about the eyelet when pointed at; occasion photos drift inside their frames as the row scrolls; the boutique's arched photo opens gently from narrow to full width as it scrolls in; the Styled by You prints arrive with a fade and a slight rise as the section scrolls in (CSS scroll-driven); the promo banner's heading slides across the hem as it scrolls in, its letters turning from ivory to ink as they cross (CSS scroll-driven, plays back on scroll up; unsupported browsers show the finished state).
- Calm by default: no looping or attention-seeking motion (no marquees, card shine sweeps, magnetic buttons or pulsing dots). The announcement bar shows one message at a time with a soft fade. One exception, by design: the hero wall breathes: its photo (window light and leaf shadows) drifts a few pixels with a slight skew over 14s and back, as if a breeze moves the leaves (`.hero::before`; the readability wash above it stays still; off with reduced motion).
- Cross-page: native view transitions. A gold thread draws across the top of the screen and sweeps down, unveiling the new page behind it while the old one settles back (600ms; `.page-thread` in Base.astro, visible only during a transition); the product photo still morphs from card to product page above it. Reduced motion: no transition.
- Easing `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; 150–300ms for feedback; ≤ 900ms for the focal entrance. Reduced motion: no spatial movement, keep fades and state changes.
