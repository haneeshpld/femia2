# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Women in Kerala, roughly 22–40 (working women and young professionals), browsing mostly on phones. They buy everyday and festive ethnic wear (kurtas, co-ord sets, dresses, tops, bottoms) and may also visit the boutique in person. Confirmed with the owner, 15 Nov 2026.

## Product Purpose
Femia is a Kochi boutique selling contemporary ethnic wear. The storefront lets a shopper browse, choose size and colour, and pay (UPI, one gateway, Cash on Delivery or WhatsApp order); the owner runs everything from Admin Studio on a phone. Success at launch: homepage to order placed in under 90 seconds on a phone; owner adds a product in under 3 minutes.

## Positioning
A real boutique on MG Road, Kochi, with a curated edit rather than a marketplace catalogue: shoppers can feel the fabric in person, chat on WhatsApp, and buy online within Kerala.

## Operating Context
Kerala-only delivery at launch (pincode check). Free shipping above ₹999. Easy returns within 7 days. Prices in rupees. The owner edits homepage text and photos from Admin, so every section must look right with owner-supplied content.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Astro storefront on Cloudflare free tier; Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90 performance, ≥ 95 accessibility/SEO/best practices; WCAG AA; 44px tap targets; reduced motion respected. Femia sells women's dresses and kurta-style wear, **not sarees**. Coupons, Malayalam UI and interstate shipping arrive in 1.1.

## Brand Commitments
Name Femia; emblem (crowned woman in a gold ring) and calligraphic wordmark in `design/reference/`. Approved mockups in `design/reference/` set the palette (maroon, cream, blush, gold), the arch motif and the silk-ribbon hero. Copy says "Add to Bag".

## Evidence on Hand
Mockups and logo files in `design/reference/`. No real product photography, testimonials or press yet: the current catalogue is dev placeholder cropped from the mockups and must not be presented as real.

## Product Principles
1. The boutique is real: Kochi, the shop, WhatsApp and the people behind it are the proof.
2. Phone first, every time.
3. Owner-editable without breaking the look.
4. Fast before fancy: richness never costs the 90-second checkout.
